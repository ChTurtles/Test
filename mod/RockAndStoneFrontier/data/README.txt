Rock and Stone Frontier writes here while you play:
  drg_stats.lua    gear stats read from your own Deep Rock Galactic install (made by tools/rsf-extract.exe)
  drg_extract.log  what the extractor found in your DRG files
  rsf.log          what the mod did in Far Far West (class picks, damage routes, input actions)
Delete drg_stats.lua to read your DRG files again, for example after a DRG update.
